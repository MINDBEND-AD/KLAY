## Decisions needed from the PO

1. **Signalsmith pitch accuracy (task 1.6 deviation).** Length changes keep a sine's pitch within ±2 cents, but
   Signalsmith's length-preserving pitch shift misses a pure sine by up to ~8 cents (default preset) and ~20 cents
   (cheaper preset). Key Track uses this pitch shift for the transposition cache. Options: accept it (judge in LT-2),
   or build pitch shifts as a Signalsmith time-stretch by the pitch ratio plus a Repitch by the same ratio. That
   gives exact pitch but different artefacts.
2. **Level at 8× stretch.** Up to ~3.5 dB is lost on a sine at 8× (default preset), or from 2× with the cheaper
   preset. Is that acceptable, or should the renderer compensate?
3. **Stretch knob in Repitch mode.** The spec does not say what it does. The current behaviour is a pitch-preserving
   length change (Signalsmith) first, then the pitch knob as varispeed. Confirm or correct.
4. **Factory samples for 1.17 / LT-1.** Where and when do the 10–15 parts arrive?
